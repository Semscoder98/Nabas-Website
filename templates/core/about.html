Origin & Heritage

A Legacy of Solidarity, Learning and Cultural Pride

The National Association of Badagry Students (NABAS) was birthed in 1991 at Obafemi Awolowo University through the vision and determination of students of Badagry origin, led by Dr. Akande M. Aina, Mr Opa Peter, Mr Akran Tope and other progressive minds. What began as a unifying platform for Badagry students quickly evolved into a movement dedicated to promoting the rich heritage, culture, and identity of Badagry, while also advancing the welfare and academic interests of its members.

By 1992, NABAS began expanding beyond its founding campus. A vibrant chapter was established at Lagos State University (LASU), Ojo, marking the association’s strong presence within Lagos State. In that same year, another branch was successfully established at University of Ibadan, further extending NABAS’ reach and influence beyond Lagos. The momentum continued as the association spread to other tertiary institutions including Adeniran Ogunsanya College of Education - AOCOED (now Lagos State University of Education - LASUED), Lagos State Polytechnic - LASPOTECH (now Lagos State University of Science and Technology - LASUSTECH), and several other institutions.

A Season of Transition

During the 2020/2021 academic session at Lagos State University, in a bid to align with the structure of National Union of Lagos State Students (NULASS), which recognizes the five divisions in Lagos State, the association expanded its scope to accommodate students from other local governments within Badagry Division. This development led to a change of name to the National Association of Badagry Division Students (NABADS), and consequently, the temporary suspension of NABAS as previously known.

Resuscitation and a New Dawn

In 2022, hope was rekindled. Through the intervention and support of key stakeholders, including the then Executive Chairman of Badagry Local Government, Hon. Adeniran Onilude, and the Supervisor for Education, Hon. (Mrs.) Sewede Pot-Balogun, alongside dedicated students, a Town hall meeting was convened to chart a new course.

From that historic Town hall meeting a Caretaker Committee was constituted under the leadership of Comr. Ajape Abdusomad Ajibade as Chairman, with the mandate to reposition the association and conduct credible elections.

In June 2023, a congress was held, which led to the formation of an Electoral Committee Chaired by Comr. Makinde Endurance Whenayon. On 24th December 2023, a landmark election was successfully conducted, producing the first set of executives after the association’s resuscitation. The leadership, headed by Comr. Gbeyon Ayomide Sewenu as President and Rt. Hon. Abel Senemede as Speaker, signaled a renewed era of purposeful leadership.

The executives were officially inaugurated on 4th March 2024. A day that marked not just an inauguration, but a rebirth. It symbolized resilience, unity, and the unwavering spirit of Badagry students.

Moving Forward

Today, NABAS stands stronger — rooted in heritage, united in purpose, and driven by a commitment to academic excellence, cultural preservation, and student welfare. From its founding in 1991 to its resuscitation in 2022, NABAS remains a testament to the enduring power of unity and visionary leadership.

Purpose & Compass

What Drives Our Mission

Our Vision

To be a preeminent student association recognized nationally for cultivating exemplary scholarship, unyielding unity, and sustainable civic and socio-economic transformation across Badagry Division and Lagos State.

Our Mission

To unite, support, and empower Badagry students across all tertiary institutions through responsive advocacy, merit scholarships, mentorship, career readiness initiatives, and impactful grassroots community outreach.

The Foundations of NABAS

Our Five Core Pillars

PILLAR 01

Unity & Brotherhood

Fostering deep camaraderie among Badagry students across diverse campuses, towns, and communities.

PILLAR 02

Academic Integrity

Promoting uncompromising scholastic excellence, research, peer-to-peer tutorials, and study bursaries.

PILLAR 03

Cultural Preservation

Celebrating Badagry’s rich maritime legacy, Ogu traditions, historical landmarks, and indigenous values.

PILLAR 04

Transformational Leadership

Training students with hands-on governance, ethics, public speaking, and project management capabilities.

PILLAR 05

Student Welfare & Advocacy

Standing as an assertive voice for student rights, campus safety, accommodation access, and financial relief.

Historical Footprints

The Journey So Far

How a visionary student gathering blossomed into one of Lagos State's most active and recognized student associations.

Formation

The Inception of NABAS

Visionary student pioneers from Badagry across Lagos institutions convened to birth a unified front to champion the academic welfare and collective interests of indigenous students.

Expansion

Decentralized Campus Chapters

Establishment of accredited institutional chapters at LASU, AOCOED (now LASUED), UNILAG, and YABATECH, bringing representative leadership closer to students.

Social Impact

Bursary Support & Free JAMB Forms

Launched the historic educational support scheme, providing preparatory tutoring and free UTME forms to secondary school leavers across Badagry division.

Today & Beyond

Digital Innovation & Global Network

Scaling nationwide representation to 20+ tertiary institutions, digital portals for bursary access, and international career mentorship networks for alumni.

Checks & Balances

Organizational Structure & Governance

Student Executive Council (SEC)

Led by the President, the SEC executes policy decisions, oversees association operations, manages programmes, and represents Badagry students in national affairs.

Executive Arm

Student Parliamentary Council (SPC)

The legislative pillar made up of chapter delegates and parliamentarians who debate policy, ratify budgets, uphold constitutional integrity, and protect student interests.

Legislative Arm

Institutional Campus Chapters

Autonomous campus executive boards in universities, polytechnics, and colleges responsible for member onboarding, localized welfare, and academic tutorials.

Campus Grassroots

Advisory Council & Patrons

Distinguished elders, royal fathers, prominent Badagry leaders of thought, and past alumni who provide strategic counsel, mentorship, and institutional stability.
