About NABAS

Who We Are

The National Association of Badagry Students (NABAS) is a non-political, non-profit organisation that brings together Badagry indigenes and residents in tertiary institutions across Nigeria.

Learn More

Our Vision

To be a leading student association known for excellence, unity and sustainable development.

Our Mission

To unite, support and empower Badagry students through advocacy, capacity building and impactful programmes.
