Is Your Institution Not Yet Accredited?

Any tertiary institution in Nigeria with up to 15 indigenous Badagry students is constitutionally eligible to inaugurate an accredited collegiate chapter of NABAS.

STEP 01

Form Interim Caucus

Convene Badagry indigenes on your campus and elect a 3-member interim committee (Coordinator, Secretary, and Welfare Lead).

STEP 02

Submit Verification

Apply through the National Secretariat with student ID copies and faculty verification confirming collegiate standing.

STEP 03

Parliamentary Ratification

The Student Parliamentary Council (SPC) ratifies your charter, presents the official chapter seal, and swears in your chapter delegates.
