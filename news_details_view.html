NABAS Leadership Secures Enhanced Tertiary Bursary Quota for Badagry Indigenes

The Central Executive Council of the National Association of Badagry Students (NABAS) is pleased to announce a landmark breakthrough regarding the special educational subvention scheme for indigenes across accredited tertiary institutions in Nigeria.

“Our administration vowed that educational and economic barriers must never truncate the scholastic potential of any Badagry youth. Today's subvention ratification is testament to constructive student diplomacy and steadfast engagement.”
— Comr. Boyon John Metonno, National President, NABAS

Following consultative sessions convened with statutory education boards and youth advancement stakeholders, the special tertiary subvention quota has been expanded by 35% for the forthcoming academic session. Priority allocations have been formally designated for undergraduate scholars in medicine, engineering, agricultural technologies, and indigent students.

All indigenous students across federal and state universities, polytechnics, and colleges of education are encouraged to keep their local government certificates and student identification documents ready. Online verification clinics will commence in the coming weeks under the direct supervision of the National Secretariat.
