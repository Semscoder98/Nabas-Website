Origin & Heritage

A Legacy of Solidarity, Learning and Cultural Pride

The National Association of Badagry Students (NABAS) was established out of a collective aspiration to provide an institutional umbrella for all students of Badagry origin studying across universities, polytechnics, and colleges of education across the federation.

Rooted in the illustrious coastal history of Badagry—a cradle of early Western education, resilient diplomacy, and cultural distinction in West Africa—NABAS connects tertiary students across institutions such as LASU, UNILAG, LASUSTECH, LASUED, YABATECH, and federal universities nationwide.

Beyond campus borders, NABAS serves as the vanguard for youth advocacy, educational interventions, bursary access, and civic engagement, making sure the collective voice and potential of Badagry's next generation of leaders are heard and nurtured.

Purpose & Compass

What Drives Our Mission

Our Vision

To be a preeminent student association recognized nationally for cultivating exemplary scholarship, unyielding unity, and sustainable civic and socio-economic transformation across Badagry Division and Lagos State.

Our Mission

To unite, support, and empower Badagry students across all tertiary institutions through responsive advocacy, merit scholarships, mentorship, career readiness initiatives, and impactful grassroots community outreach.

The Foundations of NABAS

Our Five Core Pillars

PILLAR 01

Unity & Brotherhood

Fostering deep camaraderie among Badagry students across diverse campuses, towns, and communities.

PILLAR 02

Academic Integrity

Promoting uncompromising scholastic excellence, research, peer-to-peer tutorials, and study bursaries.

PILLAR 03

Cultural Preservation

Celebrating Badagry’s rich maritime legacy, Ogu traditions, historical landmarks, and indigenous values.

PILLAR 04

Transformational Leadership

Training students with hands-on governance, ethics, public speaking, and project management capabilities.

PILLAR 05

Student Welfare & Advocacy

Standing as an assertive voice for student rights, campus safety, accommodation access, and financial relief.

Historical Footprints

The Journey So Far

How a visionary student gathering blossomed into one of Lagos State's most active and recognized student associations.

Formation

The Inception of NABAS

Visionary student pioneers from Badagry across Lagos institutions convened to birth a unified front to champion the academic welfare and collective interests of indigenous students.

Expansion

Decentralized Campus Chapters

Establishment of accredited institutional chapters at LASU, AOCOED (now LASUED), UNILAG, and YABATECH, bringing representative leadership closer to students.

Social Impact

Bursary Support & Free JAMB Forms

Launched the historic educational support scheme, providing preparatory tutoring and free UTME forms to secondary school leavers across Badagry division.

Today & Beyond

Digital Innovation & Global Network

Scaling nationwide representation to 20+ tertiary institutions, digital portals for bursary access, and international career mentorship networks for alumni.

Checks & Balances

Organizational Structure & Governance

National Executive Council (NEC)

Led by the National President, the NEC executes policy decisions, oversees association operations, manages programmes, and represents Badagry students in national affairs.

Executive Arm

The Senate & Students' Parliament

The legislative pillar made up of chapter delegates and senators who debate policy, ratify budgets, uphold constitutional integrity, and protect student interests.

Legislative Arm

Institutional Campus Chapters

Autonomous campus executive boards in universities, polytechnics, and colleges responsible for member onboarding, localized welfare, and academic tutorials.

Campus Grassroots

Advisory Council & Patrons

Distinguished elders, royal fathers, prominent Badagry leaders of thought, and past alumni who provide strategic counsel, mentorship, and institutional stability.
